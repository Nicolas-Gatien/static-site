Biological Computing

2026-02-27

Biological Computing is the field of study which explores the using biological parts in computers. This field has long been of interest to me, but I haven't developed my knowledge to a very deep degree. For the time being I will be developing this page as a repository of areas that I want to explore further, and also as a sort of wiki for other people interested in biocomputing. This page is in constant construction. The main purpose is to have an accessible entry into biocomputing, while also linking to the resources I think are good in this area.

Biological Computing is a very broad field, and includes many sub-categories such as:

  1. neuromorphic-computing
  2. Slime Mold Computing
  3. DNA Computing
  4. Brain Organoid Computing
  5. Biological Neural Networks
